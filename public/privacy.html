Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Version 1.2

Publication note: This policy describes the current RETURNO application based on its implementation. The campus privacy owner must confirm the official data controller/contact, hosting and email providers, applicable retention schedule, and legal wording before RETURNO is shared beyond an approved test.

1. About this policy

RETURNO is a campus lost-and-found application. This policy explains what information the application asks you to provide, how the application uses it, and who can see it. The institution operating a particular RETURNO deployment must identify the responsible data controller and provide its approved privacy contact before public release.

2. Information RETURNO handles

Account information: your full name, email address, password hash, account role, account creation time, and policy acceptance record. Passwords are stored as password hashes, not in readable form.

Lost-and-found reports: report kind and status, item name, date and time, location, description, optional image, and contact details you choose to add, such as an email address, phone number, or other contact text. Report information and contact details are visible to authenticated RETURNO users, including students and administrators. Do not include information you do not want shared with signed-in users.

Outcome proof: a student who reports that an item was claimed or returned must upload an image showing the handover. This image is private and can be viewed only by administrators reviewing that request. When an administrator confirms or dismisses the request, RETURNO removes its active database reference and asks the private storage provider to delete the image. A provider cleanup failure is logged and may require operator follow-up. Pending evidence stays in the live system until an administrator reviews it; backup copies may remain under the operator's backup-retention schedule.

Account and service records: session records, short-lived email-verification and password-reset records, and student requests to delete an account. Verification and reset codes expire after 10 minutes. The application stores a hash of the session token rather than the token itself.

3. How information is used

The application uses account information to register users, confirm registration email addresses, sign users in, recover passwords, and enforce student and administrator permissions. It uses report details to display, search, and manage lost-and-found posts and their outcomes. It uses deletion requests to let administrators review an account-closure request.

When email delivery is configured, RETURNO sends registration verification codes, password-reset codes, and password-change notices to the relevant account email address. The application source for this version contains no product analytics integration.

4. Who can access or receive information

Authenticated students and administrators can see reports and the contact details attached to them. Administrators can also see registered users' names and email addresses, manage reports, process account-deletion requests, and review private outcome-proof images. Students cannot access those proof images. The application does not provide students with other users' account profile pages.

The RETURNO operator must configure the host and SMTP mail provider. Their identities and privacy terms depend on the deployment and are not determined by this source code. The configured mail provider receives the recipient address and the content needed to deliver RETURNO email messages. The hosting provider may process connection and application traffic. The operator must identify these providers and explain any other disclosures required for the deployed service.

5. Browser storage

RETURNO uses an HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie for sign-in; production deployments must enable the Secure cookie setting and HTTPS. The browser's session storage remembers the selected user role for the sign-in and navigation interface. The application source does not include advertising or product-analytics cookies.

6. Storage and retention

The current application stores its database in a local SQLite file managed by the server. A hosted deployment may use different infrastructure; the operator must identify it. Account and report information has no automatic age-based deletion period in this version. An administrator's account deletion removes that account and its associated reports and sessions from the live database through database relationships. A student's deletion request is reviewed by an administrator and does not itself delete the account.

SQLite backups can contain personal information and are separate copies. Deleting information from the live database does not by itself erase existing backups. The operator must set and publish a backup-retention and deletion schedule and protect backup files. This project does not set a universal retention deadline.

7. Security and limits

The application uses password hashing, session controls, server-side role checks, and validation of submitted data. These implementation measures do not guarantee that every deployment, device, network, host, or backup is secure. The deployment operator is responsible for configuring HTTPS, restricting database and backup access, protecting mail credentials, and managing security incidents.

8. Your requests and questions

Use the account controls to request account deletion. An administrator reviews that request; if approved, the account and associated reports are deleted from the live database, while backup copies may remain until the operator's backup schedule removes them.

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact the privacy office or data-protection contact designated by the institution operating your RETURNO service. The official contact and the process and timelines for applicable privacy rights must be confirmed and published by that institution.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RETURNO or its deployment changes. The version and update date appear above. New registrations record the policy version accepted during registration. The institution operating RETURNO must decide how to notify existing users and whether renewed acceptance is required for a particular change.

10. Deployment details to complete before public release

  • Institution-approved name of the data controller and privacy contact.
  • Actual hosting provider and SMTP/email provider for the deployed service.
  • Approved retention periods for accounts, reports, operational records, and backups.
  • Institution-approved privacy-rights process, legal basis, and final legal review.
